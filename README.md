
## User Role Privileges

### Buyer

* Browse products
* Search products
* View product details
* Purchase products
* View previous orders

### Seller

* View own products
* Add products
* Edit own products
* Delete own products

### Business Account Administrator

* View business users
* View business information
* View products belonging to their business

### Agora Master Admin

* View marketplace statistics
* View all users
* View all businesses
* View all products
